## Overview

![WashGo Booking Flow](/assets/image/1_washgo.png)
*The WashGo booking flow, including the optional AI dirt analysis upload step.*

**WashGo** is a booking web app for on-demand home-service vehicle washes. A customer fills in their details, picks a package (Exterior Only or Bundling), chooses an available time slot for their region, and gets a WhatsApp confirmation.

Built as a 6th-semester Software Engineering (RPL) team project on **Laravel 11**, **Blade**, **Bootstrap 5**, **SQLite/MySQL**, and the **Google Gemini API**.

## My Role: AI Dirt Analysis Integration

My part of the team built the **AI Dirt Analysis** feature. Customers can upload a photo of their car before booking, and my code sends that image to Gemini, reads the verdict, and feeds it into the booking total. Three layers, end to end:

- **Frontend capture**: The booking form has an optional photo upload step. No photo is a valid choice; the rest of the form works either way.
- **Gemini service**: A small `GeminiService` wraps the call to `gemini-2.0-flash-lite`, sends the uploaded image as base64, and parses the model's structured response back into the application.
- **Price and time mapping**: The verdict becomes one of three levels (`LIGHT`, `MEDIUM`, `HEAVY`), and each level maps to a fixed surcharge (+Rp 0 / +Rp 15.000 / +Rp 30.000) plus extra service time (+0 / +15 / +30 minutes) that the rest of the booking sees as a normal line item.

The single photo is the only AI-driven input; everything downstream (admin view, WhatsApp message, total price) is plain Laravel.

## Full Feature Set (Team Project)

**Customer Side**
- Online booking form (name, email, phone, region, date, time, address)
- Package selection: Exterior Only (Rp 60.000) / Bundling (Rp 100.000)
- Auto-filtered available time slots based on date + region
- Auto-formatted `+62` phone numbers for WhatsApp deep links
- Optional AI dirt analysis photo upload

**Admin Dashboard**
- Real-time booking list (auto-refresh every 5 seconds)
- Status workflow: Pending → Confirmed → Completed / Canceled
- Per-booking AI dirt level badges
- One-click WhatsApp confirmation with prefilled message
- Schedule, price, and booking management

## What I Learned

The prompt design was the actual hard part, not the API call. My first version asked Gemini a free-form question and got back paragraphs of mixed honest prediction and speculation about the car's brand, color, and location. Switching to a strict JSON schema with allowed values (`light` / `medium` / `heavy`) plus a system prompt that told the model to ignore everything outside the car fixed it; the same `GeminiService` then returned parseable results on every call. Lesson: when a model's output feeds business logic, the prompt is part of the contract.
